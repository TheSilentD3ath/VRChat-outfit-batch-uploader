# Interface

v3.3 organizes the window around the work you are doing instead of placing every control on one long page.

## Outfits

The main workspace shows every detected outfit as a compact card.

Always visible:

- outfit name and active/stored state,
- batch inclusion,
- platform summary,
- **Select**, **Ping**, and **Upload**,
- Contacts, Lights, Parameters, and estimated VRAM.

Expand the card to edit its Blueprint ID and platform targets or open the secondary tools: VRAM optimization, thumbnail update, blendshape overrides, item selection, and FaceEmo assignment.

Performance warnings use a restrained warning color so the list remains readable. Hard limits, invalid IDs, and genuine blockers remain red.

## New Outfit

This workspace filters the list to outfits without a Blueprint ID. Their **Express** and **Advanced** first-upload actions remain visible even while the rest of the card is collapsed.

## Defaults

Shared settings have their own scrollable workspace:

- avatar name, description, release, and content-warning defaults,
- thumbnail source and background,
- SDK-fix and consent behavior,
- texture/VRAM optimization,
- item defaults,
- project-data export and import.

## Batch footer

The batch summary, dry run, sound option, progress, cancellation, and main upload action stay below the scrolling list. They no longer move out of reach below the defaults page.

## Tips

- Keep most cards collapsed while selecting the batch.
- Expand only the outfit you are configuring.
- Use **New Outfit** when adding fresh uploads and **Defaults** only when changing the shared Express workflow.
